## 2. 3 Research Tracks 구조 (현행 — v1 modified)

### ① fMRI Foundation Model
- **데이터**: UK Biobank (5만 명 fMRI in 10만 명 MRI cohort) + HCP, Narratives, NSD
- **비전**: brain FM × **LLM agentic AI 로드맵** ("AI Scientist for Brain" — 중장기 지향, PI 명시 disclaimer 있음)
- **Lab papers**: NeuroMamba (NeurIPS Workshop, Published), Scalable Diffusion Transformer 4D fMRI (NeurIPS Workshop, Published), SwiFT V2 (ongoing), MBBN (arXiv)
- **NLM KB**: SwiFT V2 (124 src), MBBN (156), Agentic LBM (136), World Model × LBM (103)

### ② Ephys Foundation Model
- **데이터**: spike / LFP / EEG / iEEG, multi-species
- **비전**: ephys × **LLM agentic AI 로드맵** (BCI next layer — 신경 활동 → 자연어 reasoning → 자율 실험)
- **Lab papers**: DIVER-1 (arXiv:2512.19097), EEG Memory × DIVER (under review), LBM × Qualia iEEG (in prep for ICLR 2027)
- **NLM KB**: NF-Mouse Ephys FMs (47), DIVER-1 ICML Rebuttal (180), LBM × Qualia (68)

### ③ Continuous Neural Field Modeling
- **차별화**: multi-scale × multi-species × multi-modality (①② 트랙이 못 푸는 frontier)
- **Lab + 협업**: OmniField (K. Valencia et al., **ICLR 2026 main**, arXiv:2511.02205), Mamba-GINR (NeurIPS Workshop), STACI (NeurIPS)
- **NLM KB**: NF-Mouse GINR (8 src), NF-Mouse SSL Strategies (31), NF-Extend AI4Science (153)
- **Position**: "Lab next big frontier — 학부생 first cohort 합류 자리"
